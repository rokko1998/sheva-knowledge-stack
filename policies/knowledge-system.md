# Shared knowledge policy

Canonical knowledge lives in Obsidian Brain. ObsidianDataWeave is the controlled write and knowledge-maintenance layer. FTS5 is the default local retrieval layer before any canonical write. AI Brain in NotebookLM is a curated semantic projection, not a second source of truth.

AI Brain consists of stable logical sources: CURRENT_CONTEXT, PROJECTS, DECISIONS, EXPERIMENTS, RESEARCH_INSIGHTS, PEOPLE, and LESSONS_LEARNED. Each source is rebuilt from current canonical state. Historical publication snapshots are not canonical knowledge.

Preserve durable knowledge rather than silently losing it. Never infer facts unsupported by a candidate, its evidence, or retrieved canonical context. Do not call a candidate duplicate before retrieval. Jev classifies an action; it does not write semantic text. `cloud_safe=false` always means AI Brain bucket NONE. Insufficient context means UNCERTAIN or REVIEW. Confidence and probabilities are telemetry until separately calibrated.
